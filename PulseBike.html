Frequently Asked Questions

How does the hybrid power system work? ▼

The Pulse Bike features three power modes: pedal-only, electric motor-only, or a combination of both. When you pedal, the kinetic energy charges the battery automatically, making it a self-sustaining system. You can switch between modes using the handlebar controls.

Is the semi-autonomous mode safe? ▼

Absolutely! The semi-autonomous system uses advanced sensors and AI to assist with navigation and collision avoidance. You always maintain steering control, and the system acts as an intelligent co-pilot. The sensors work 24/7 and can detect obstacles in complete darkness using night vision technology.

What happens if someone tries to steal the bike? ▼

The Pulse Bike has multiple anti-theft features: it emits an extremely loud alarm, sends instant notifications to your phone, activates the GPS tracking system, and all cameras start recording. The bike can be remotely locked via the app, making it immobile. Additionally, the movement sensors detect unauthorized handling immediately.

How does the airbag protection work? ▼

The airbag system uses advanced sensors to detect imminent collisions or falls. Within milliseconds, airbags deploy to protect both the rider and critical bike components. The system provides comprehensive 360° protection, including head, torso, and limb protection for the rider, as well as shock absorption for the bike frame and electronics.

What features does the mobile app offer? ▼

The Pulse Bike app is your complete control center: real-time GPS tracking, remote lock/unlock, route navigation, user permission management for shared access, battery status monitoring, ride statistics, maintenance reminders, and access to recorded camera footage. You can also customize bike settings, update firmware, and contact emergency services directly from the app.

What's included in the warranty? ▼

Every Pulse Bike comes with a comprehensive 3-year warranty covering all electronic components, battery, motor, sensors, and structural frame. We also provide free software updates for life and 24/7 technical support. The warranty includes one free annual maintenance checkup at our service centers.
