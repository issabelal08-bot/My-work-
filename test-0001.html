📚 School Do’s and Don’ts 📚

DO’S

Arrive on time

Be punctual for classes, assemblies, and school events to show respect and responsibility.

Participate actively

Ask questions, join discussions, and contribute to group activities to enhance your learning.

Respect teachers and peers

Treat everyone with kindness, listen without interrupting, and value diverse opinions.

Keep the campus clean

Dispose of trash properly and help maintain a tidy, healthy environment for everyone.

Wear the uniform properly

Dress according to school guidelines to promote unity and discipline.

Complete assignments on time

Develop good study habits by meeting deadlines and putting effort into your work.

DON’TS

Don’t use phones in class

Keep devices away during lessons unless permitted by the teacher for educational purposes.

Don’t cheat or plagiarize

Academic integrity is vital — always submit your own work and give credit where due.

Don’t bully or harass

No form of teasing, discrimination, or intimidation is tolerated — be an ally, not an attacker.

Don’t run in hallways

Walk calmly to avoid accidents and keep traffic flowing safely.

Don’t bring prohibited items

Weapons, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, or electronic devices not allowed per school policy are forbidden.

Don’t skip classes or homework

Absenteeism and neglect harm your progress and disrupt the learning environment.
